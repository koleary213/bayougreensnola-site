Good to Know

Wedding questions

Can the holes and golf balls be personalized?
Yes. Our Custom Package adds personalized holes with your names, logo, or colors, plus 2 dozen custom-printed golf balls your guests can take home, starting at $200. Please order early, since custom printing takes lead time.
Can you set up indoors or outdoors at my venue?
Both. We need a reasonably flat, clean surface, about 4 ft x 10 ft per hole. Tell us about your venue and we will design the course to fit the space.
How far ahead should we book for a wedding date?
The earlier the better, especially for weekend dates, which fill up fast. Book with extra lead time if you want custom-printed golf balls.
What if it rains?
Mini golf plays best in dry weather. If rain is in the forecast, we will work with you on a plan, such as moving the course indoors or under cover where possible, or finding another date.
